EXPLANATION:
  pf 'block drop' silently discards packets → client retransmits SYNs
  until timeout. Nothing listening → RST → instant 'connection refused'.
  Timeout IS the firewall fingerprint. pf is stateful: 'pass' creates
  state, so edge→backend replies flow back without a separate outbound rule.
  Cloud analogue: security groups / NACLs (private subnet pattern).
